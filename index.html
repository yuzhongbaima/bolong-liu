Dr. Bolong Liu is a researcher at the College of Intelligence Science and Technology, National University of Defense Technology, and also conducts cooperative postdoctoral research at the School of Mechanical Engineering, Beijing Institute of Technology under Prof. Changle Xiang, Member of the Chinese Academy of Engineering.

Research interests include cross-domain mobile platforms, hydrodynamic optimization, CFD-based design, energy-saving mechanisms, data-driven configuration optimization, and intelligent control, with applications to water-land amphibious platforms and water-air cross-domain systems.

Selected Research Projects

  • Principal Investigator, National Natural Science Foundation of China project on data-driven high-speed configuration optimization of amphibious platforms.
  • Principal Investigator, Basic Strengthening Program project on key technologies for folding-wing air-water cross-medium vehicles (CNY 2.0 million).
  • Principal Investigator, China Postdoctoral Science Foundation project on data-knowledge co-driven configuration optimization and maneuvering-performance coupling mechanisms for non-streamlined amphibious platforms (CNY 80,000).

  • Sub-project contributor, MIIT key project on multifunctional emergency rescue platforms for complex water-land scenarios.
  • Core contributor to multiple national and ministerial-level research programs related to cross-domain platform technology and amphibious mobile systems.
